About MealBridge

Connecting surplus food with people who need it.

MealBridge is a community-focused food sharing platform designed to reduce avoidable food waste.

Restaurants, households, hotels, institutions and event organizers can donate surplus edible food.

NGOs and volunteers can find available food, submit requests, collect meals and distribute them to people in need.

The platform promotes responsible food consumption and encourages communities to share surplus meals.
